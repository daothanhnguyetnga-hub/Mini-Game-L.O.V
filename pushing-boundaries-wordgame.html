🚀 WORD PEEK

This letter grid hides 5 keywords related to the event theme.
Each word connects adjacent cells (up/down/left/right – no diagonals), and each cell belongs to exactly one word.

How to play: Pick the color that matches the keyword you want to solve. Then, on the word board below, drag across the cells to highlight the word you find.
📸 Once you're done, take a screenshot and submit it via the [MS Form submission link – to be added] for a chance to receive an event ticket.
You need at least 3 out of 5 correct words to qualify. Your submission order and number of correct words determine your priority for receiving a ticket to the event.
